Rates Include

  • Private air-conditioned transport pickup & drop-off across Lombok Island.
  • 1-night accommodation (homestay/hotel) before the trek.
  • Licensed English-speaking guide and experienced local porters.
  • Complete camping equipment: Double-layer tent, warm sleeping bag, mattress, toilet tent, folding chairs.
  • 3 full meals daily, fresh tropical fruits, hot coffee/tea, biscuits, and bottled mineral water.
  • Mount Rinjani National Park entry tickets & climbing insurance.

Rates Exclude

  • Personal porter to carry private daypack / luggage ($20 - $25/day).
  • Tipping for mountain guide and porter team.
  • Personal outdoor gear (Trekking shoes, warm down jacket, headlamp, poles).
  • Flight or speed boat tickets to/from Lombok Island.
