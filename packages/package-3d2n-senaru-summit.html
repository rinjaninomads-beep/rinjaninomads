3D2N Senaru – Lake – Summit

3 Days / 2 Nights • The Grand Reverse Circuit

Package Overview

Prefer shaded jungle walks first before the high-altitude summit push?
This reverse traverse ascends through the cool canopies of Senaru, descends directly to Segara Anak Lake for hot-spring relaxation on Day 2, and saves the iconic 3,726m summit sunrise for the final morning.

Our expert local team handles campsite management, daily fresh meals, porterage, and trekking logistics to provide a seamless expedition.

  • Start Point: Senaru Village (601m)
  • Finish Point: Sembalun Village (1,156m)
  • Max Altitude: 3,726m (The Summit)
  • Level: Challenging & Strenuous

Detailed Itinerary

Day 1 Senaru Village – Senaru Crater Rim (Camp 1)

Highlight: Shaded tropical canopy & rim sunset view of Mt. Baru Jari.

  • 07:30 AM: Registration at Senaru Rinjani Trek Center.
  • 08:00 AM – 12:00 PM: Climb through tall tropical rainforest; hot lunch served at Pos 2.
  • 13:00 – 16:30 PM: Continue upward past Pos 3 to Senaru Crater Rim (2,641m).
  • 17:30 PM: Tents ready with sunset views across the volcanic lake. Dinner and overnight.
  • Meals: Lunch, Dinner.

Day 2 Senaru Rim – Segara Anak Lake & Hot Springs – Sembalun Rim

Highlight: Natural volcanic hot springs soak & campsite prep at Sembalun Rim.

  • 07:00 AM: Early breakfast at the rim.
  • 07:30 – 10:00 AM: Descend the rocky path into the caldera to Segara Anak Lake (2,000m).
  • 10:00 AM – 12:30 PM: Bathe in the therapeutic hot springs (Aik Kalak) and lunch at the lake.
  • 13:00 – 16:30 PM: Hike up the crater wall to Sembalun Crater Rim (Camp 2 - 2,639m).
  • 18:30 PM: Dinner and early sleep for the summit attack.
  • Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner.

Day 3 Summit Attack (3,726m) – Sembalun Village

Highlight: Pinnacle sunrise victory & gentle grassland descent.

  • 02:00 AM: Midnight wake-up call, hot tea/coffee, and light snacks.
  • 02:30 – 06:00 AM: Conquer the ridge to Rinjani Summit (3,726m) for sunrise.
  • 08:00 AM: Return to Sembalun Camp for warm full breakfast.
  • 09:30 AM – 14:00 PM: Walk down through open savannas to Sembalun Village (lunch at Pos 2).
  • 14:30 PM: Private vehicle transfer to Senggigi, Kuta Lombok, or Airport.
  • Meals: Breakfast, Lunch.
