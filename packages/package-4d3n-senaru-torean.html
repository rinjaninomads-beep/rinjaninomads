4D3N Senaru – Lake – Torean

4 Days / 3 Nights • Leisure Lake Discovery & Canyon Wonders (No Summit)

Package Overview

Want a pure wilderness holiday without the exhaustion of climbing the peak?
This 4 Days 3 Nights leisure trek eliminates the steep midnight summit climb. Instead, you'll immerse yourself in Senaru's dense rainforests, spend plenty of restorative time relaxing and fishing by Segara Anak Lake, and trek through the jaw-dropping gorge of Torean.

With complete camping equipment and full meal support from our Guides and Porters, it’s the best way to experience Rinjani at an enjoyable, leisurely pace.

  • Start Point: Senaru Village (601m)
  • Finish Point: Torean Village (585m)
  • Max Altitude: 2,641m (Senaru Crater Rim)
  • Level: Moderate / Leisure Adventure

Detailed Itinerary

Day 1 Senaru Village – Senaru Crater Rim (2,641m)

Highlight: Forest biodiversity & sunset over Mount Agung.

  • 07:30 AM: Registration at Senaru RTC.
  • 08:00 AM – 12:00 PM: Trek through tropical rain forests with lunch served hot at Pos 2.
  • 13:00 – 16:30 PM: Emerge at Senaru Crater Rim (Camp 1 - 2,641m).
  • 17:30 PM: Unobstructed sunset views over Segara Anak Lake and Mount Baru Jari. Warm dinner at camp.
  • Meals: Lunch, Dinner.

Day 2 Senaru Crater Rim – Segara Anak Lake

Highlight: Down into the crater, natural thermal pools & camping by the water.

  • 07:30 AM: Sunrise breakfast at the rim.
  • 08:30 – 11:30 AM: Descend the winding cliff path towards Segara Anak Lake (2,000m).
  • Afternoon: Camp setup right at the lakeshore. Free afternoon for swimming in natural hot springs, fishing, or photography.
  • 19:00 PM: Warm lakeside campfire dinner under starlit skies.
  • Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner.

Day 3 Segara Anak Lake – Torean River Valley

Highlight: Relaxed morning at the lake & entering the dramatic Torean gorge.

  • Morning: Leisurely wake-up, hot coffee, and peaceful lakeside strolls.
  • 11:30 AM: Hot lunch before setting off on the scenic Torean route.
  • 12:30 – 16:30 PM: Trek alongside cascading mineral streams and giant mountain cliffs.
  • 17:00 PM: Camp 3 nestled inside the Torean valley forest.
  • Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner.

Day 4 Torean Valley – Torean Village

Highlight: Waterfalls, hanging bridges & village farming fields.

  • 07:30 AM: Jungle breakfast and final camp pack-up.
  • 08:30 AM – 12:30 PM: Gentle downhill trek along waterfalls and local agricultural fields to Torean Gate.
  • 13:00 PM: Lunch at base, retrieval of storage bags, and transfer to your next hotel, port, or airport.
  • Meals: Breakfast, Lunch.
