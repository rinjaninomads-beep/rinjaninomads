4D3N Sembalun – Lake – Torean

4 Days / 3 Nights • Summit & The Dramatic Jurassic Park of Lombok

Package Overview

Want a more relaxed pace with cinematic valley sceneries?
This 4 Days 3 Nights itinerary combines the grandeur of the Summit (3,726m) with extra leisure time at Segara Anak Lake, finishing through the breathtaking Torean trail featuring sheer canyon walls, roaring waterfalls, and mineral springs.

Led by our Professional Guides and Porters, you’ll enjoy a well-paced trek with delicious meals, top-tier camping tents, and zero rush.

  • Start Point: Sembalun Village (1,156m)
  • Finish Point: Torean Village (585m)
  • Max Altitude: 3,726m (The Summit)
  • Level: Moderate to Adventurous

Detailed Itinerary

Day 1 Sembalun Village – Sembalun Crater Rim

Highlight: Grassland savannah hike and crater rim camp.

  • 07:30 AM: Registration at RIC Sembalun.
  • 08:30 AM – 12:30 PM: Trek through savanna grasslands to Pos 1 and Pos 2; lunch at Pos 3.
  • 13:30 – 17:00 PM: Ascend Bukit Penyesalan to Sembalun Crater Rim (Camp 1 - 2,639m).
  • 18:30 PM: Hot dinner and early rest overlooking Segara Anak Lake.
  • Meals: Lunch, Dinner.

Day 2 Summit (3,726m) – Lake Segara Anak Camping

Highlight: 360° Sunrise from Summit & relax at lakeside camp.

  • 02:00 AM: Midnight wake-up and snack before summit push.
  • 06:00 AM: Arrive at Summit (3,726m) to watch the golden sunrise across Indonesia.
  • 09:00 AM: Descend back to camp for full breakfast.
  • 10:30 AM – 13:30 PM: Steady descent into the caldera to Segara Anak Lake (2,000m).
  • Afternoon: Pitch tents lakeside. Enjoy hot mineral springs, fishing, and sunset camp fire.
  • Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner.

Day 3 Segara Anak Lake – Torean Valley Camp

Highlight: Leisure lake morning, White River (Kaliputih) & giant canyon vistas.

  • Pagi: Relaxed lakeside morning with coffee, photo sessions, or second hot spring dip.
  • 11:00 AM: Start trekking along the Torean gorge route.
  • 13:30 PM: Lunch near Kaliputih river crossing under hanging canyon walls.
  • 16:30 PM: Set up Camp 3 in the Torean forest area (Propok / Gua Susu area).
  • Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner.

Day 4 Torean Jungle – Torean Village

Highlight: Jungle waterfalls, lush rain canopy & farming village finish.

  • 07:30 AM: Breakfast amidst birdsongs in the rain forest.
  • 08:30 AM – 12:30 PM: Gentle descent through tropical rainforests and local plantations to Torean Gate.
  • 13:00 PM: Lunch at basecamp, luggage pick-up, and private transfer to airport, harbor, or hotel.
  • Meals: Breakfast, Lunch.
