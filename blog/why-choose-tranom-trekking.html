Why Choose Tranom Trekking?

About Us • Safety, Community & Sustainable Climbing

Selecting the right trekking agency is the single most critical choice for your Mount Rinjani journey. Tranom Trekking combines local heritage, safety ethics, and high hospitality standards.

Certified Guides & Fair Porter Welfare

Our mountain guides are native Sasak locals with formal wilderness safety training and years of high-altitude experience. We guarantee strict load weight limits, fair wages, quality shoes, and warm sleep setups for our porters.

Deluxe Mountain Campsite Comfort

Rest is fuel for the body at high altitude. We supply double-layer wind-proof tents, thick sponge mats, cold-rated sleeping bags, private sit-down toilet tents, and folding dining tables with chairs.

Fresh Mountain Menus

Say goodbye to boring instant noodles. Our field chefs serve hot, nutritious meals prepared freshly three times a day: Indonesian fried rice, chicken curry, fresh tropical fruits, hot ginger tea, and ground Lombok coffee.

Zero-Waste & Clean Mountain Pledge

Every single piece of plastic and campsite trash generated during our tours is packed out back to our Senaru base for proper recycling.

Climb with Locals

Experience Mount Rinjani guided by trustworthy mountaineers.
