The Ultimate Mount Rinjani Guide

Everything you need to conquer the 3,726-meter pinnacle safely

Updated September 2026 10 min read By Tranom Mountaineering Team

Standing at 3,726 meters above sea level, Mount Rinjani on Lombok Island is the second-highest volcano in Indonesia[cite: 1]. It rewards climbers with emerald crater lakes, steaming volcanic springs, and an unforgettable sunrise above the clouds[cite: 1]. However, it is an authentic alpine challenge that requires serious respect and thorough planning.

"The summit attack from Sembalun Crater Rim is notorious: for every two steps forward on deep volcanic sand and scree, you often slide one step back. Endurance and mental grit are paramount."

1. Choosing Your Trekking Route

There are three primary trekking trails to navigate the Mount Rinjani volcanic massif:

Sembalun Route (The Summit Highway)

Starting at Sembalun Village (1,156m), this route meanders through expansive, open grasslands before climbing Bukit Penyesalan (The 7 Hills of Regret) toward the Sembalun Crater Rim (2,639m)[cite: 1]. It is the mandatory starting point for anyone aiming for the fastest path to the 3,726m summit[cite: 1].

Senaru Route (The Tropical Rainforest)

Starting lower at Senaru Village (601m), this path is shaded by heavy jungle canopy until reaching the Senaru Crater Rim (2,641m)[cite: 1]. It is famous for gentle walks and unobstructed panoramic sunsets across Segara Anak Lake and Mount Baru Jari[cite: 1].

Torean Route (The Scenic Canyon)

Recently popularized as the "Jurassic Park" trek, this trail connects Segara Anak Lake to Torean village via sheer cliff passages, dramatic hanging bridges, and cascading mineral waterfalls[cite: 1]. Highly recommended as a descent route on 4-day expeditions[cite: 1].

2. Elevation Breakdown & Key Campsites

  • Sembalun Base: 1,156m ASL[cite: 1]
  • Pos 2 Tengengean (Lunch Stop): 1,500m ASL[cite: 1]
  • Sembalun Crater Rim (Basecamp Summit Attack): 2,639m ASL[cite: 1]
  • Mount Rinjani Summit Peak: 3,726m ASL[cite: 1]
  • Segara Anak Caldera Lake & Hot Springs: 2,000m ASL[cite: 1]
  • Senaru Crater Rim: 2,641m ASL[cite: 1]

3. Essential Packing Checklist

Your guide and porters carry large communal gear (tents, mats, cooking gear, food supplies, and water)[cite: 1]. In your own 20–30 liter personal backpack, you must pack:

Clothing

  • Windproof down jacket
  • 2-3 quick-dry hiking shirts
  • Thermal base layer (night)
  • Gloves & warm beanie
  • Rain poncho / shell

Hardware & Shoes

  • Broken-in trail shoes
  • Headlamp + extra batteries
  • Trekking poles (crucial)
  • Dust mask / buff scarf
  • UV sunglasses & hat

Personal & Hygiene

  • High SPF Sunscreen 50+
  • Blister tape / plaster
  • Electrolyte rehydration tabs
  • Personal medications
  • Power bank for phone

4. How to Tackle Summit Night

Summit attack begins at 02:00 AM[cite: 1]. You will face approximately 1,100 vertical meters of climbing in total darkness and near-freezing temperatures. Keep these rules in mind:

  1. Small, rhythmic steps: The final 400 vertical meters along the loose scree ridge are grueling. Do not rush; keep a steady breathing rhythm.
  2. Protect against windchill: Winds along the ridge can gust up to 40 km/h. Put on your windproof outer layer, warm gloves, and a buff around your face before leaving camp.
  3. Stay hydrated: The dry mountain air dehydrates you quickly. Keep water bottles inside your pack insulation so they do not become freezing cold.

5. Leave No Trace Principles

Mount Rinjani is a sacred sanctuary for the local Sasak and Balinese people. Help us protect this delicate alpine ecosystem: carry all non-biodegradable waste down to basecamp, respect quiet hours at campsites, and avoid using soap directly in Segara Anak lake water.
